# CourtPass — Admin Guide

Platform Admin Guide for CourtPass management operations.

## 1. Venue Approval & Forced Deactivation
- **Venue Approvals:** Review pending venue submissions. Approve or Reject with a mandatory reason.
- **Forced Deactivation:** Deactivating a venue automatically cascades to cancel all future pending/confirmed bookings at that venue, notifying impacted customers.

## 2. Coach Identity Verification
- Inspect uploaded NIC document photo artifacts submitted by coaches.
- Mark coach identity status as `verified` or `rejected`.

## 3. No-Show Dispute Resolution
- Review customer no-show dispute claims.
- Clearing a no-show dispute automatically recalculates the customer's reliability score and tier in real time.

## 4. User & Review Management
- Deactivate or reactivate user accounts across all roles (Customer, Owner, Coach).
- Remove policy-violating reviews or announcements.
