# CourtPass — Coach Module Guide

The Coach Module allows independent sports coaches to host public or private training sessions at approved venues.

## 1. Coach Registration & NIC Photo Verification
- Register via the dedicated **Join as Coach** page.
- Upload a photo of your **NIC document** on your dashboard.
- *Note:* To protect PII, no NIC numbers are extracted or stored. Admin verifies the photo artifact.

## 2. Requesting Venue Approval
- Browse active venues and submit an approval request to venue owners.
- Once approved by the owner, you can schedule sessions on that venue's courts.

## 3. Creating Training Sessions
- Create **Public** (discoverable by all players) or **Private** (shareable link with token) sessions.
- Session creation automatically conflict-checks against existing court bookings and other coach sessions.
- Set student capacity and price per student.

## 4. Session Cancellation & Student Refunds
- Need to cancel a session? Cancelling automatically issues a 100% full refund to all registered students and frees the court block.

## 5. Coach Reviews & Tag System
- Students can leave verified reviews after attending a session.
- Reviewers self-select a **Parent** or **Player** tag.
- You can post 1 public response per review.
