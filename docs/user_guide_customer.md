# CourtPass — Customer User Guide

Welcome to CourtPass! CourtPass makes booking indoor sports courts across Sri Lanka simple, reliable, and fast.

## 1. Creating an Account
- Click **Sign Up** on the top right.
- Fill in your name, email, phone number, and password.
- New accounts start in the **New Member** reliability tier.

## 2. Browsing & Booking Courts
- Select a venue or filter by sport (Futsal, Badminton, Pickleball, Squash, Billiards, Carrom, Table Tennis).
- Choose a date and view real-time available 1-hour slots.
- **Payment Methods:**
  - **Online Payment (PayHere Sandbox):** Available for all members. Instant confirmation via credit/debit card or mobile wallet.
  - **Cash on Arrival:** Unlocked automatically once you achieve **Standard+** tier (5+ completed bookings and 70%+ reliability score).

## 3. Managing Bookings & Cancellation Policy
- Go to **My Bookings** to view your upcoming and past reservations.
- **Cancellation Policy:**
  - **> 48 hours before slot:** Full 100% refund (online) or Responsible cancellation (cash).
  - **12–48 hours before slot:** 50% refund (online) or Moderate penalty (cash). Or list for resale!
  - **< 12 hours before slot:** No refund or Irresponsible penalty.

## 4. Resale Marketplace
- Have a confirmed online booking you can't attend? List it on the **Resale Marketplace**.
- Server enforces a price cap of **90% of the original booking price**.
- Cash-on-arrival bookings can be converted to online payment before listing for resale.
- Unsold listings can be revoked at any time.

## 5. Sports Diary & Leaderboards
- Check **My Sports Diary** to see your total games played, venues visited, and sport breakdown.
- View the **Most Active This Month** leaderboard to compete with players at your local venue.
