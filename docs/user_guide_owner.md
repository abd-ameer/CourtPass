# CourtPass — Venue Owner Guide

CourtPass helps indoor venue owners maximize court utilization, manage booking requests, and gain customer intelligence.

## 1. Venue Registration & Approval
- Sign up as a **Venue Owner**.
- Submit your venue details (Name, Address, City, Phone, Description).
- Your venue enters `pending` status until reviewed and approved by Platform Admin.

## 2. Managing Courts & Operating Hours
- Add courts under your venue (specify sport type and hourly rate).
- Set operating hours per court (e.g., 08:00 AM to 10:00 PM). Slots are automatically generated on-the-fly.

## 3. Booking Requests & Customer Intelligence
- View pending booking requests on your **Owner Dashboard**.
- Click **View Intelligence** on any request to inspect the customer's reliability score, past no-show count, and history at your venue.
- Confirm or Reject booking requests (rejections require a mandatory reason).

## 4. Flash Deals
- Have an unbooked upcoming slot today or tomorrow? Mark it as a **Flash Deal** with a discounted price to attract instant bookings.

## 5. Approving Coaches & Announcements
- Review coach requests to operate at your venue. Approve or decline with feedback.
- Post Operational (maintenance) or Promotional (weekend discount) announcements per venue.
- View peak-hour court utilization heatmaps powered by Chart.js.
